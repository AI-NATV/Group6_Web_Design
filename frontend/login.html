ABOUT THE PROJECT

A mini BI workspace built around product price data.

Upload a CSV, review how its fields are understood, explore the data through interactive charts, and forecast a selected product series in one place.

01

Understand your data

Review price history, spot missing values and duplicates, and confirm the meaning of each column.

02

Compare forecasting techniques

Evaluate Naive, moving-average, damped Holt and optional seasonal or Ridge models using chronological validation, MAE and RMSE.

03

Make analysis more useful

Give users a clear way to visualize trends, inspect forecast errors, and keep their datasets and dashboards organized.

Forecasts are estimates for exploration—not guarantees. Model performance is shown on historical holdout data.
